We pour our beer by the glass, filled fresh from the brewery taps. There is room inside and a
large outdoor **bier garten**.

### Beer to take home

We sell beer to go in:

- 32oz and 64oz growlers
- 750ml bottles
- 32oz Crowlers
- 12oz cans (6-packs)
- 16oz cans (4-packs)
- Kegs

### Food

Now featuring **Double D's Sourdough Pizzeria**, fresh sourdough pizza right in the tasting room.

### Book the room

We can hold part of the tasting room or the bier garten for a group, or the
whole place if you need it. The building holds 88.
