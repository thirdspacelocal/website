The Third Place

Craft the reason to gather.

Gravity is a third place — the spot between home and work where the neighborhood comes together. The beer is award-winning and the pizza is worth the trip, but those are really just the excuse. The point is the table you pull up to, and the people already sitting there.

Inspirational Explorations

Why “Gravity”?

The name says it in one word. It's the pursuit of high-quality, full-flavored ales and lagers — a product of substance and dignity; the adventure lifestyle we love here in Colorado; a sense of our place in this world; and a little thought provocation with every pour.

Not to mention, gravity is the term brewers use to measure the density of wort and beer — and so its strength. We stay endlessly curious, always looking to science and space exploration for inspiration.
